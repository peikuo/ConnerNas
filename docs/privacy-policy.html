CornerNAS Privacy Policy

Effective date: February 26, 2026

CornerNAS is a local-network file sharing app that helps you turn an old device into a simple NAS on your home or office LAN.

CornerNAS is designed for local use. Files are shared directly on your local network and are not uploaded to a CornerNAS cloud service.

1. Information We Access

  • Folders/files you explicitly choose to share on your device.
  • Local network information needed to discover devices and serve files on your LAN.
  • Basic app settings you choose (for example language, preferred port, device name).

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect or transmit your personal data to our servers through the CornerNAS app. CornerNAS does not require an account and does not provide a cloud backend for file storage.

3. How Your Data Is Used

  • To let you browse, upload, download, copy, and move files between your devices.
  • To advertise and discover CornerNAS devices on your local network.
  • To remember local app preferences and selected shared folders on your device.

4. Data Sharing

CornerNAS shares files only with devices that can access your local network and connect to your CornerNAS device. We do not sell your data and we do not share it with advertisers.

5. Permissions Used

  • File/Folder access: to access folders you select for sharing.
  • Network / Internet access: to serve files and discover devices on your LAN.
  • Foreground service / notifications: to keep the server running reliably and let you stop it.

6. Security

CornerNAS is intended for LAN-only use. Android and iOS builds are designed to operate on local networks. You are responsible for your local network security (for example, Wi-Fi password and trusted devices).

7. Children

CornerNAS is not specifically directed to children under 13. If your distribution targets children, you should provide an appropriate privacy policy and disclosures for that use case.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will be posted at this URL with an updated effective date.

9. Contact
